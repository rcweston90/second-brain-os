# 6-archive

**Purpose:** Cold storage — done, abandoned, or untouched >12 months. Out of sight, not deleted.

**Subfolders:**
- `projects/` — completed/abandoned project folders
- `writing/` — given-up drafts
- `legacy-structure/` — cruft swept during a past reorganization, kept for recovery

**Rule:** Archive, don't delete. Nothing here is browsed routinely; it exists for recovery and audit.

**Lifecycle question this folder answers:** *Is this retired?*
