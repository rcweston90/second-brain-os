# _meta/research — the brain's research journal

**Purpose:** research *about how this second brain should work* — deep-research runs, evidence syntheses, and investigations into the system's own design. The vault studying itself. Distinct from articles you consume and from decisions you've made.

**Why it's here and not in the library:** a clipping is someone else's article you consume (and might productionize into your own writing). A doc here is *yours, about the system* — you commissioned it, it synthesizes many sources, and its job is to inform how the vault runs. Different genre, different lifecycle.

**The boundary, three ways:**

| Genre | Home | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Clipping** | `3-library/clippings/` | external article, consumed; may become an essay in `4-studio/` |
| **Research** | here | *your* investigation about the system; cites sources but is a synthesis, not one source; feeds decisions |
| **Decision** | `_meta/IA-decisions/` | the *choice* the research led to — research is the evidence, the ADR is the verdict |

The chain runs research → decision: an evidence synthesis informs an architecture decision record, which the operating docs then point to.

**Lifecycle question this folder answers:** *Is this research about how the brain itself should work?*
