# 3-library/clippings

**Purpose:** web clippings — articles and posts *someone else produced*, saved to consult. Each gets a `## My take` stub on capture. A clipping worth building on gets productionized into your own writing in `4-studio/writing/`.

**What does NOT belong here:** your own research *about how the vault / second brain works*. That's a different genre — it lives in `_meta/research/`, not the library.

The test when unsure:
- *Did someone else write this as an article I'm consuming?* → **clipping** (here).
- *Did I commission this to understand my own system?* → **`_meta/research/`**.
- *Is it the decision that research led to?* → **`_meta/IA-decisions/`**.

**Convention:** append `## My take` — the note isn't done until the take is real. The operational dashboard flags clippings whose take is still an empty stub.

**Lifecycle question this folder answers:** *Is this someone else's article I consult?*
