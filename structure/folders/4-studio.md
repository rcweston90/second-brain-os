# 4-studio

**Purpose:** Your original output — things you make.

**Contents:**
- `writing/` — staged pipeline: `seedlings/ → drafts/ → ready/ → published/`. Movement between stages (a frontmatter property) is the signal, not topic.
- `sketches/` — raw personal ideas, half-formed thinking
- `templates/` — content templates
- `apps/` — app concepts / specs

**Rule:** If you authored it, it's here. Consumed reference goes to `3-library`. Move writing forward by stage as it matures — exactly one essay sits in `ready/` at a time.

**Lifecycle question this folder answers:** *Am I the one creating this?*
