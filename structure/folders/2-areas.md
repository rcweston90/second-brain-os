# 2-areas

**Purpose:** Ongoing responsibilities and identity — no finish line. Things you maintain a standard in, not complete.

**Contents:**
- `identity/` — personal philosophy, values, beliefs, the decision layer, credentials (kept in a never-read `codes/` subfolder)
- `career/` — portfolio, branding, case studies, work notes, evidence log
- `lists/` — books, resources, reference lists
- `topics/` — **the wiki**: one hub page per recurring subject — your position in your own words plus links to the projects and library notes that are the evidence

**Rule:** If it's something you *are* or *steward* indefinitely, it's here. If it has a finish line, it belongs in `1-projects`.

**Lifecycle question this folder answers:** *Is this an ongoing part of who I am or what I maintain?*
